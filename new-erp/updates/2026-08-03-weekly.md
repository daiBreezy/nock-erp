Weekly Update (3 – 7 Aug 2026)

Last Week
- I was only in for 2 days last week (used personal leave), so overall progress was lighter than usual.
- Where I did spend ERP time, it split roughly 90% Bus Route / 10% Billing. On Bus Route: reworked it onto a new route-based model, added auto multi-round handling when a van goes over capacity, backward-calculated pickup times off the class schedule, and a live departure queue for daily ops. On Billing: payment-matching against bank statements and a course-schedule picker for invoicing — both still in progress.
- Worth recapping why Billing keeps pulling focus across so many things at once: it's the biggest bottleneck in the whole system. Almost every other module's data flows into it to produce one Invoice — Course + Teacher + Class + Session + Student + Calendar + Bus Route (and in Phase 2: Family, Inbox, CRM) — plus all the logic hanging off it (Package, Price, Book fee, learning dates, pickup/dropoff, suggested class/session, promotions, etc). It's the most complex, messiest part of the system right now, and it'll matter even more to the Director once it becomes a report — it's the real bottleneck on the Academy finance side.
- MJ is still in Phase 1 dev, syncing regularly with Shibasan. Dev has been slower than usual because nearly every sync surfaces new feature requests or logic/flow changes from MJ. On top of that, since real Billing dev is still a way off, Shibasan asked MJ to prioritize an automation flow for invoice creation to help Admin in the meantime — Liclass month-end renewals can hit 80-100 invoices/month, which is brutal for Admin, so automating that buys time before the real system gets built.
- Arm shipped the Personalize Goal feature 2 weeks ago — strong signups (1,600+ users), but only 3 completed the full flow. Likely cause: tracking isn't granular enough to show where/why users drop off. Also handling a small-but-noisy Admin issue: users are confused about whether they're still Premium. Arm's first fix (a status tag under Profile) turned out to be the wrong spot — Shibasan wants it on the More page instead (not finalized yet). A good example of a tiny feature with an outsized impact.
- Main friction this week: hard to find a consistent time to sync as a team, and communication between MJ and Shibasan isn't continuous.
- Admin side: I'm currently out of the loop on status — lost contact with Pin, who was on leave last week. Following up this week.
- A pattern I noticed with the team: they often know what they want but can't explain the reasoning to Shibasan, and don't dig for context to bring back options. Examples — Attendance→Payroll: if Jobcan doesn't work on price, they're ready to drop it instead of comparing other vendors. e-WHT: nobody called KBank directly to get details. Expense sheet: the person who owns it doesn't feel able to break the old flow to build the one they actually need — even though they're the one who has to compile everything downstream for KMD — and chose to keep the old format instead. There are plenty of paths forward on that one and nobody's taking them; genuinely confusing to me.
- Followed up with Jobcan on Attendance→Payroll: they can technically do everything we need, but three dealbreakers — price too high, support is a one-time free setup call only (no ongoing support), and the UX/UI is rough. Now evaluating alternatives: Human Soft and Empeo look feature-complete on paper, with free after-sales support and more reasonable pricing.

This Week
- Human Soft demo, 4 Aug 14:00-14:30 via Google Meet — evaluating as a Jobcan alternative for Attendance/Payroll.
- Reconnect with Pin to get Admin status aligned after last week's gap.
- Continue Billing work and support MJ/Shibasan on the invoice-automation flow.

Key Insight
- The team tends to work strictly within whatever scope they've been handed — if told to look at only X, they'll only look at X. That's the real problem: not a lack of capability, but a missing habit of asking "why" and bringing options back to the team instead of stopping at the first blocker.

Team Building
- Admin (Pin) was on leave last week — reconnecting this week to catch up.
- MJ and Arm continuing regular syncs with Shibasan, though communication continuity between them is still a friction point.

Note
- Billing's parallel workstreams (bank-statement matching, course-schedule picker, enrollment-frequency logic) aren't scope creep — they're symptoms of it being one large bottleneck problem.
- Personalize Goal (Arm): 1,600+ signups, only 3 completions — investigating drop-off tracking before deciding next steps.
