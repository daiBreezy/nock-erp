Problems
- Most of the work is taking in input from every member and building a shared understanding. Some issues are misunderstandings of how to use the system. Some are requests for more than the system does today. Some members can't put what they need into words. So we have to sit down, reset everyone's basic understanding of the system and work through the workflow again, or add to it, on many topics.

Key Insight: Communication
- Any piece of information needs both sides' understanding. Once you find the middle ground in how to explain it, everyone can understand it correctly.
- Trying something for real teaches far more than sitting through an explanation without doing it.
- Receiving a message is an art, translating it is an art, and communicating is an art. There's no fixed rule that makes a topic land with a particular person.
- Getting one person to understand something doesn't mean the next person will understand it the same way.
