Weekly Update (12 – 16 Oct 2026)

Last Week
- ERP Data Import: Most of the week was focused on importing existing data into the ERP. We faced several challenges, especially because each branch used a different Google Sheets template, and aligning everyone's understanding required a lot of time and effort. However, the long-term benefits are worth it. Instead of asking every branch's Admin to recreate all Students, Courses, and Classes from scratch, MJ and I created standardized templates for them to fill in and import directly into the ERP.
  As the final step, we asked the Admins to complete the Session Sheet so we could link Sessions to Students, Courses, and Classes. This would give us usable, up-to-date data and allow the branches to continue their work immediately. We worked through Sunday because Nockacademy made time for us on both Saturday and Sunday. I really enjoyed the process!
- SWAG (Company Goods): Khun Ing started printing the second prototype last Friday. We expect to receive the physical sample around the middle of this week. Once Shibasan approves it, we can confirm the design and proceed with the full print order.
- e-WHT: The bank has approved our application. We will attend our first meeting with KBank on Wednesday, October 14, to learn how the system works and understand how to apply it to our future workflows.
- Fire Safety Training: I attended four hours of fire safety training on Friday and learned a lot about electrical safety. For example, Thai Industrial Standards (TIS) certification may cover the power cable, the plug, or both, so we need to check exactly what is certified when purchasing electrical products. I also learned the recommended sequence for connecting a power strip: plug the device into the power strip first, then plug the power strip into the wall outlet. Another surprising lesson was that switching off a power strip does not necessarily disconnect the electrical supply completely.

This Week
- ERP Data Import: Continue focusing on data imports and linking records so the branches can use the ERP immediately. Student, Course, and Class imports are already working. The Session import is the only remaining piece to complete this goal.
- SWAG: Follow up on the second prototype and get Shibasan's approval before confirming the full print order.
- e-WHT: Attend the KBank meeting and continue learning how to use the system.
- ERP Go-live & Operations: Starting October 15, I expect to receive a significant amount of feedback from all branches after launch. We also need to prepare for month-end operations, particularly consolidating invoices and receipts from every branch for KMD. This process currently remains split into two parts: data collected before the ERP rollout and data recorded after the system goes live.

Key Insight
- Data standardization requires cross-team alignment: Getting everyone to follow a consistent data structure takes considerable time and effort, especially when people have different priorities and levels of cooperation. However, establishing a shared template now will reduce duplicated work and make the ERP more sustainable in the long run.
- Learn from what already works: Khun Tum, the Area Manager, has done an excellent job planning the data sheets for the Chonburi branches. His approach could serve as a reference for other branches. In comparison, the Bangkok data sheets may not have received enough attention, which could explain why their templates are more fragmented.
- Recognize people who make collaboration easier: Admin Dear from the Bangna branch provided excellent support by coordinating information, collecting data, and helping pass work between people. Although these tasks were not necessarily part of her direct responsibilities, she handled them very well. Her support made a real difference, and I truly appreciate it.
