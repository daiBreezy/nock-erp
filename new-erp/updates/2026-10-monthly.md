Monthly Update — Oct 2026

Highlights
- The ERP went live for every branch. Before launch, MJ and I spent most of a week importing what the branches already had. Every branch kept its Google Sheet differently, so we built one standard template for Students, Courses and Classes, and then a Session Sheet to link them together. We worked through the weekend, with Nockacademy giving up their Saturday and Sunday. The launch meeting was almost last-minute (Shibasan and I each thought the other was setting it up), but more than 17 people joined and it went smoothly.
- Billing is complete. The last 10% (every realistic scenario, including Master Admin) is closed.
- Feedback is moving. After launch I opened a Feedback Sheet. Of 49 items, MJ has fixed 36 on Staging and I've covered 42 in ERP V2. The first 6 priority items from launch: 4 are fixed, and 2 are still in progress (what "First timer" means, and a student's full class history).
- e-WHT is in use. KBank approved us, we had the first meeting, the Token arrived (by way of our Tuek Nam branch) and is with Shibasan, and an Admin has started using it. For now they also write a paper copy of every slip until they trust it.
- SWAG: the second clicker prototype was approved, paid for and ordered. We're now waiting on Shibasan for the final wording. We've also started a Liclass version, with Khun Ing's team doing the 3D work from the start.
- Web App: School pages are done for all 8 branches using real data, the Landing page is rebuilt, and the website and mobile app are now separate so each can grow on its own.

Progress vs Plan
- Plan from September: (1) finish the last 10% of Billing · (2) import before 15 Oct, then go live · (3) keep V2 one phase ahead while MJ starts Phase 2 · (4) e-WHT access and learning · (5) Brand CI decision + bring the SWAG clicker cost down.
- (1) Done. Billing is closed.
- (2) Done, and bigger than planned. The import landed on time, and we opened the ERP to every branch instead of only the three pilot branches.
- (3) V2 is ahead (42/49 feedback items). MJ has started part of Phase 2 (Inbox > Form > CRM), but the launch and feature fixes still come first, so Phase 2 may run late.
- (4) Ahead of plan. We went from applying for access to real use, with a paper backup for now.
- (5) SWAG moved to a print order. Brand CI is still waiting on Shibasan.
- Also this month: started the Report Page with Shibasan (Overview tab drafted, waiting for confirmation), and I attended 4 hours of fire safety training.
- Where we slipped: Phase 2. Supporting a company-wide launch takes MJ's time first.

People & Team
- Admin Dear (Bangna) coordinated data and passed work between people, well beyond her role. It made a real difference.
- Khun Tum (Area Manager, Chonburi) planned his branches' data sheets so well that they can be the model for other branches.
- The team showed up for the launch even though it was arranged at short notice.
- 1on1 with Shibasan: my Self Evaluation and OKR review came out positive. Shibasan asked me to do more to lift energy and attitude across the company.

Key Insights
- Standardizing data takes alignment across teams, and that is slow. But one shared template now saves every branch from rebuilding later.
- Learn from what already works. Chonburi's sheets were the cleanest, and they should be the reference.
- Clear ownership prevents last-minute surprises. Even with people you work closely with, confirm who is doing what.
- Going live is not the same as adoption. Some branches have used the ERP for days and still get stuck. People need to understand the flow, not just have access.
- Trust in a new tool is built, not assumed. Running e-WHT next to paper is slower, but it's the right call for now.

Next Month Focus (November)
1. ERP meeting with every user: talk through problems with those already using it, and a full recap/training for those who aren't. Use it to listen, not just to teach.
2. Close the remaining feedback items with MJ (13 open on Staging).
3. Keep Phase 2 (Inbox > Form > CRM) moving alongside launch support, with V2 one phase ahead.
4. e-WHT: support the Admin until the paper backup can stop.
5. SWAG: confirm the clicker wording and finish the order. Agree on price and the 3D model for Liclass.
6. Report Page: finish the Overview tab with Shibasan, then move to the next tabs.
7. Month-end for KMD: invoices and receipts are split between before and after go-live.

Risks / Decisions Needed
- Adoption: if branches don't understand the flow, they'll fall back to the old sheets. The November meeting is the main fix.
- Phase 2 may be late while MJ's time goes to launch support and fixes.
- Month-end data is split across sheets (before go-live) and the ERP (after go-live), which makes KMD consolidation harder.
- Waiting on Shibasan: clicker wording, Report Page logic and time, and the Brand CI direction.
- e-WHT double work (online + paper) costs Admin time until confidence is there.
